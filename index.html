About the shop

Stylish cuts. Friendly faces.

We're a small neighbourhood barbershop on Windsor Street — a couple of chairs, a good radio, and enough chat to keep the queue moving. Whether you want a clean fade, a long-overdue beard tidy, or your usual short back & sides, we'll get you sorted and back out the door.

Walk in, or give the shop a ring on the day. We'll always try to fit you in.
